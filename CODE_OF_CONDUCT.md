# Code of conduct

ClipKeeper is a small project, and everyone who comes here — to report a bug, ask a question or send a fix — should feel
welcome. English and Russian are both fine.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient. People come with different setups, experience and languages.
- Talk about the problem, not the person. Disagreeing is fine; disagree with reasons.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Respect people's time: a clear report with steps and a log helps more than a long one.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, personal attacks, slurs, or demeaning jokes — about anyone.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves.
- Posting someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, or repeatedly pushing a topic after a maintainer has closed it.

## Where it applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space of this project, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainer, [@Alukkart](https://github.com/Alukkart), privately — through the contact on the GitHub profile —
or, if it happened in a discussion, use **Report content** in GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may ask someone to stop, edit or remove comments, lock a thread, or block a
person from the project for a while or for good. Decisions are made as fairly as possible, and the maintainer explains
them when that's safe to do.

In the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
